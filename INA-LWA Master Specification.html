INA LIFE & WEALTH ACADEMY

Master Specification

An Ethiopian online learning, training and professional development network. Learn • Build • Apply • Grow

Student → Member → Certified Learner → Instructor → Mentor → Academy Partner

Five systems

1. Online learning platform

Student dashboard: My Courses, Continue Learning, Certificates, Membership, Assignments, Exams/Quizzes, Progress, Live Classes, Instructor, Payment History, Community, Profile.

2. Training and live classes

Self-paced: Module → Lesson → Video → PDF → Quiz → Assignment → Final Exam → Pass → Certificate. Live (Zoom/Meet): Mon lesson, Wed workshop, Fri Q&A, Sun assignment review.

3. Membership

Free, Basic, Pro, Premium/Master. Pricing is a starting test; adjust after market testing.

4. Certificate and verification

Course completion → assessment → pass → certificate ID → public verification.

5. Instructor network

Apply, review, approve, certify. Revenue sharing and career levels.

Certificates and verification

Certificate contents

  • Student name, course name, level
  • Completion date
  • Certificate ID, e.g. INA-LWA-2026-000125
  • Instructor and Academy Director
  • QR code and verification URL

Verify at ina-lwa.com/verify/INA-LWA-2026-000125

Six certificate types

  • Course Certificate
  • Professional Certificate (several courses)
  • Training Certificate (live training)
  • Instructor Certificate
  • Academy Diploma (long program)
  • Master Certificate (advanced program)
